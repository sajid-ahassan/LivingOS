# Access Policy

Policy code: ACCESS  
Version: 2  
Department: GLOBAL

1. Every employee receives a standard company email account.
2. Repository access must match the employee's assigned project and role.
3. Confidential-project access is restricted and requires IT Owner approval.
4. Confidential security training must be assigned before confidential write access.
5. Production access is not granted during initial onboarding unless separately approved by the IT Owner.
6. Access must be removed no later than the employee's final working day.
7. An AI agent may hold write access to a confidential repository only while its sponsor has completed confidential security training.
8. When an agent's sponsor changes, the IT Owner must re-certify the agent's access within 24 hours.
